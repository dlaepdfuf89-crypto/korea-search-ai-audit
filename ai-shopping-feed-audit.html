What problem it solves

Client catalogs often arrive as large CSV/TSV files where manual checking is slow, generic score checkers provide little handoff evidence, and automatic “fixes” can invent or distort merchant facts. This toolkit is designed to make that preflight repeatable without uploading the catalog to a paid API.

One Windows workflow

  • Detect known OpenAI or Google feed profiles
  • Bulk-audit CSV/TSV catalogs locally
  • Create evidence and actionable findings
  • Make only conservative formatting fixes and log every change
  • Recheck outputs and build client-ready bundles

FAQ

Does it upload my catalog?
No. Feed processing is local. Only the optional public-URL audit fetches the URL you choose.

Does Safe Fix invent missing data?
No. It performs conservative normalization and records changes; missing merchant facts still require the merchant.

Is a paid API required?
No.
